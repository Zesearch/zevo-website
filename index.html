Three properties of Zevo

  • Autonomous

    • ▸End-to-end multi-agent collaboration
    • ▸Self-improves toward your objective

  • Reliability

    • ▸Scored only by the evaluation harness you set
    • ▸The best-scoring model is what you get

  • Self-hosted

    • ▸Deploys on your own devices
    • ▸Agents can run fully sandboxed
